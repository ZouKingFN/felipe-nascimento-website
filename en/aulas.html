Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about private lessons.

Are the classes for beginners?

Yes! The classes are 100% customized for your current level, from the very first step to professional refinement.

How does scheduling work?

Scheduling is done via Google Calendar. You reserve your slot, it's automatically blocked, and you receive access instructions by email.

Where do the classes take place?

Classes take place online via Google Meet or in-person, depending on where I am in the world at the time.

Can I take classes as a couple?

Certainly! The price of a private lesson covers up to two people, making it a great option for couples or training partners to share the experience.
