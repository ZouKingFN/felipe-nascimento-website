My Specialties

As a multi-creator, I work end-to-end on the entire event experience.

Brazilian Zouk Teacher

Intensive workshops and immersive sessions focused on Fundamentals, Movement, and Brazilian Zouk Flow.

Official J&J & Party DJ

Sharp line-ups for the dance floor and technical DJ sets for Jack & Jill competitions — balancing energy and musicality.

Official J&J Judge

Technical, ethical, and certified evaluation to qualify participants in Jack & Jill competitions.

J&J Organizer

Bracket planning, heat lists, and official championship coordination at your own event.

Urban Zouk Workshops

Classes focused on musicality, dynamics, texture, and intention to transform your students' dancing.

Performances & Shows

Memorable performances blending the essence of Zouk with the attitude of urban dance styles.

Double Private Lessons

Intensive coaching with both Felipe and Nina for couples or groups seeking a transformative experience.
