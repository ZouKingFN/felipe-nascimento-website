Unique Fusion: Zouk & Hip Hop Adapted for the Floor

Many dancers try to bring urban elements into Zouk, but end up losing weight transfer or disrupting connection. In this course, Felipe & Evellyn Lethicia teach you how to extract the groove and footwork patterns from Hip Hop while adapting them with refined technique specifically for Brazilian Zouk mechanics. The result is stylish, modern, and fluid dancing.
