What do you want to train today?

Musicality
🎧 Train with:
🧠 Observe:

Pauses, energy, and peaks.

🎯 Do:

Dance focusing on transitions and interpreting silences.

Control
🧠 Focus:

Stability and axis precision.

🎯 Do:

Reduce physical force and refine movement intention. Less is more.

Connection
🧠 Concept:

React to your partner in real-time — do not anticipate memorized movements.

🎯 Practice:

Close your eyes for a few moments (if in a safe environment) and feel the weight and direction of the connection.

Fluidity
🧠 Focus:

Continuous movement. Avoid locking between sequences.

🎯 Tip:

Imagine you are dancing underwater. Each movement should generate the next one organically.
